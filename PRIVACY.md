# EyeSpy privacy policy

EyeSpy is a party game played by phones in the same room.

- **No internet.** EyeSpy never connects to the internet. Phones talk to each
  other directly over a hotspot or Wi-Fi network in the room.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.**
- **What is stored on your phone:** the name and character you pick, app
  settings, a token that lets you rejoin a game after a dropped connection,
  and (on the host's phone) a copy of the game in progress so it can resume
  after a crash. Hotspot details you type to make a Wi-Fi QR code stay on
  your phone.
- **What is shared:** your name and character are shown to the other phones
  in your game. The host's phone runs the game and never sends anyone
  another player's secret role.
- **Camera:** used only to scan a game QR code, never recorded or stored.
- **Local network:** used only to find and connect to games in the room.

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.
